about blackrhino

BlackRhino is a free, educational live flight radar for India: every aircraft over Indian airspace, on a 2D and 3D map.

What it is

BlackRhino (blackrhino.in) is a free live flight radar for India. It shows every aircraft over Indian airspace on a 2D map and a 3D map, with altitude, speed, route and airline where known.

Who it is for

People curious about flights: plane spotters, students, travellers following a flight, and anyone who wants to know which plane is above them.

Cost and purpose

It is free, with no sign-up, no ads and no subscription. It is an educational, not-for-profit project. Commercial use of the site or its data is not permitted.

Where the data comes from

Aircraft positions come from community ADS-B receiver networks (adsb.fi, adsb.lol and OpenSky). Registration, type and route come from public databases such as adsbdb. Maps come from OpenStreetMap-based tiles (OpenFreeMap), satellite imagery from Esri, terrain from open elevation data and clouds from EUMETSAT. See the credits page.

How fresh it is

Positions refresh about every 15 seconds. Between updates the position is estimated from speed and heading. Near airports, where receivers often lose the signal, landing positions are modelled and marked as estimates.

Features

Live 2D radar map; tilted 3D map and a 3D India view with terrain, sun and live satellite clouds; airport pages with live aircraft nearby; Near me, which shows which aircraft is above you using your device location, kept in your browser; search in plain words such as "hyd to blr" or "indigo above 30k ft", handled by a small language model that runs in your browser; Track a flight, which follows one flight by its number or your boarding pass and gives a link to share (the pass is read in your browser and never stored); and Share my flight, which records your phone GPS on board and lets family follow the flight.

Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics. Your location for Near me never leaves your browser. Boarding passes and tickets read on Track a flight are read in your browser: only the flight number and date are used, and nothing is stored. Details are on the privacy page.

Limits

Not for navigation, flying or safety decisions. Positions can be late, missing or wrong. Military-flagged and blocked aircraft are not shown. BlackRhino is not affiliated with any airline, airport or data provider.

Open source

The code is open source under the MIT licence: https://github.com/themonkkey/blackrhino
