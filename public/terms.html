Last updated 22 October 2026
BlackRhino is a free, non-commercial hobby service for curious people. It is not for navigation, operations, safety decisions or anything where a wrong position could hurt someone.

what you get

A live view of aircraft over India built from community ADS-B receivers, plus estimates where coverage is missing. It is free, with no account, and offered as is.

accuracy

Positions can be delayed, missing or wrong. Gaps between radar fixes are filled by dead reckoning, and landing times shown as estimates come from a model, not from the aircraft. Never rely on BlackRhino to meet or plan around a flight without checking the airline or airport.
