privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026
Short version. No accounts, no cookies, no ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels. We never sell or share data. The only personal data we store is the GPS track you choose to share with Share my flight, and it deletes itself after 7 days.

share my flight

Sharing is off until you start it and your browser asks for location permission. Your phone keeps the points on the device while offline and uploads them when it reconnects. Anyone with your share link can see the track, rounded to about 10 metres. Stop sharing at any time from the share page; everything is deleted 7 days after the share was created. To delete it sooner, email aryanjarvis32@gmail.com with the share link.

"I'm on this flight" on the radar uses your location only inside your browser and never sends it to us.

near me and the sky view

Near me is optional and stays off until you press it and your browser asks for permission. Your position, and on a phone the direction it points, are used only inside your browser to work out which aircraft is above you. They are not sent to us or to any third party, not stored and not logged. The request that loads aircraft carries no location from your device. While Near me is on, the page asks your device to keep the screen awake; that stops when you leave Near me.

ask in words (search)

The search box can turn a sentence into a filter. A small language model built for this project runs inside your browser (about 2.5 MB, downloaded from this site the first time you use it, plus a runtime library from jsDelivr). What you type is processed on your device and is not sent to us. The result is only used to filter aircraft already on your screen.

track a flight and boarding passes

Track a flight shows one flight by its flight number and date. If you upload or scan a boarding pass, or paste ticket or email text, it is read inside your browser by code served from this site. We take only the flight number, the date and the airports from it. Your name, booking reference (PNR), seat, ticket number and frequent-flyer number are never used, stored or sent, and the file and camera frames are discarded as soon as they are read. The camera is used only while the scan screen is open, after your browser asks for permission.

A track link looks like /track/6E2024?d=2026-11-05: it carries only the flight number and date, so anyone you send it to sees the flight, not you. Like any page request it appears in our hosts' standard logs. The page looks up the flight's route with adsbdb (it sends the flight number) and its position from public radar. Your recent tracked flights are kept only in your browser.

aircraft data

Aircraft positions come from public ADS-B radio broadcasts collected by community receiver networks (see Credits). Registration and type come from public databases. Aircraft owners who want their aircraft hidden can email aryanjarvis32@gmail.com with the registration or hex code.

third parties your browser talks to

To draw the map and load the app, your browser fetches files directly from these services, which therefore see your IP address: OpenFreeMap (map tiles), Esri (satellite imagery in 3D and on the live and track pages), AWS open terrain tiles, EUMETSAT (clouds, via our server), adsbdb (route and airframe lookups, sends a flight number or aircraft code), Google Fonts, and the jsDelivr and cdnjs code libraries. We don't embed social widgets or ad networks.

your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw consent (stop sharing). Email aryanjarvis32@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. The service is not directed at children under 18; Share my flight should be started by an adult.
